About

I build models that survive real data.

I’m an AI/ML Engineer at Integrity Organization Ltd and a Computer Science graduate of Landmark University. I work across the whole cycle: getting data out of PDFs, websites and spreadsheets, cleaning it, training and evaluating models, deploying them with FastAPI and Docker, and writing documentation that non-technical teams can use.

My work so far covers medical-image classification, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), workflow automation and web scraping. I publish what didn’t work alongside what did, because that’s usually where the real decisions were made.
