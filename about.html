[ WHO I AM ]

About.

I'm an AI/ML Engineer at Integrity Organization Ltd and a Computer Science graduate from Landmark University. I work across the full cycle — extracting and cleaning data, training and evaluating models, deploying them with FastAPI and Docker, and writing documentation non-technical teams can actually use. My work spans medical-imaging classification, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), workflow automation and web scraping. My final-year project combined MedCLIP image embeddings with TabNet clinical features in a late-fusion model for three-class breast-cancer diagnosis. Every project I publish documents what didn't work, not just what did.
