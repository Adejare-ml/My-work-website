[ FEATURED · FINAL-YEAR PROJECT ]

Multimodal breast cancer detection.

Looks at a breast scan and the patient’s clinical record together, and suggests one of three diagnoses.

MedCLIP turns the image into features and TabNet learns from the clinical record. Each gives its own prediction, and a late-fusion step combines them. The model is served through a FastAPI backend in Docker, covered by API contract tests.
